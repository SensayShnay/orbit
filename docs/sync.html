Message sync

An optional helper for your Mac that tells Orbit when you last talked to someone.

The short version: it reads who you messaged and when. It never reads, stores or sends what anyone said.

What it reads

  • From Messages on your Mac (iMessage and SMS): for each 1-on-1 conversation, the phone number or email, the date of each message, and whether you or they sent it.
  • From WhatsApp for Mac, if it's installed: the same three things.
  • Group chats are skipped.

What it sends

Before counting, it asks your Orbit for the phone numbers and emails of the people already in your sphere. It only sends numbers for those people: how many messages, how many you sent, the first and last date, and a count per month and year. Everyone else stays on your Mac and is never sent anywhere.

What it never does

  • Read message text, photos, attachments or reactions.
  • Send anything about people who aren't in your Orbit.
  • Change or delete anything in Messages. It reads a copy and throws the copy away.

Setting it up

  1. In Orbit, go to Settings, then Message sync, then Set up on my Mac.
  2. Copy the line it gives you and get it onto your Mac (copy and paste works across your devices, or AirDrop it).
  3. On the Mac, open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Return), paste the line and press Return.
  4. It opens System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access and shows you Orbit Message Sync in Finder. Drag it into the list and switch it on. macOS needs this permission for any app to read Messages.
  5. Press Return in Terminal. It syncs once right away, then twice a day (10:15 and 20:15) whenever the Mac is on.

Settings in Orbit shows when it last synced, or warns you if the Mac lost permission.

Turning it off

In Orbit, tap Settings › Message sync › Turn off. The Mac can no longer send anything. To remove the helper from the Mac too, paste this into Terminal:

curl -fsSL "https://mpjqxzsdjwmsfmselztl.supabase.co/functions/v1/orbit-sync?get=uninstall" | bash

Then remove Orbit Message Sync from Full Disk Access (select it and click −).
