Orbit Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Orbit is a personal app for keeping track of the people in your life. This page explains what Orbit stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What you put in

  • Your account: your email address and password (the password is stored as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Your sphere: the people you add and what you write about them, like names, circles, cities, birthdays, contact details, notes, reminders and when you last talked. You can import some of this from your phone's contacts, but only if you allow it and only the people you pick.
  • Your profile: your name, city, optional profile picture and optional details like birth time for Human Design.
  • Signals: photos and short text updates you choose to share. Each signal is deleted automatically 24 hours after you post it, along with who viewed it and any reactions.

Who can see it

  • Your sphere (the people you track and everything you write about them) is private to your account. Other Orbit users can't see it.
  • Your name, profile picture and signals are visible only to people you're connected with on Orbit. A connection happens when someone joins with your invite code, or when you both accept a connection request. You can remove or block a connection at any time.
  • When you view someone's signal, they can see that you viewed it and how you reacted.

Messages

Orbit never reads the content of your texts or chats. Message sync, an optional helper for your Mac, only counts how many messages you exchanged with someone and when, so Orbit can tell you who you haven't talked to in a while.

AI features

When you tap Draft or ask Orbit for a personal read, the details on that one person's card are sent to Anthropic's Claude to write the response. Nothing is sent unless you ask, and Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.

Where it's stored

Orbit's data is stored with Supabase, a hosting provider, on servers in the United States. It's protected so each account can only read its own data, plus the shared signals described above. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it for ads.

Notifications and badges

Reminders and birthday alerts are scheduled on your phone itself. Nothing about them is sent to a server.

Reporting and blocking

You can report a signal or block a person from the signal viewer. Reports go to the Orbit team for review, and blocked people can't see your signals or send you requests.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings, then Delete my account. This permanently erases your account, your sphere, your photos, your signals and your connections. It can't be undone.

Children

Orbit isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and we'll mention anything important in the app.

Questions? Use the Feedback option in TestFlight or the app to reach the Orbit team.
